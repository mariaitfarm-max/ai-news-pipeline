AI News — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

AI News is a free app that shows AI news headlines and a comparison of AI model prices.

Data we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect, store or share personal information. It has no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

Data stored on your device

Stories you save, your language/theme settings and a cache of the latest news are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Network requests

The app downloads a news feed and model-pricing data from our server (hosted on Cloudflare). Like any internet request, the server may technically see your IP address and request time in standard infrastructure logs; we do not use this to identify you.

Third-party content

Headlines and short snippets come from the public RSS feeds of the publishers shown in the app, and each story links to the original publisher. Model pricing data comes from OpenRouter (including benchmark data from Artificial Analysis).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.
